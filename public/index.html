About Ereto Namunyak

Rooted in Solidarity, Driven by Lasting Impact

In the Maa language, "Ereto" means mutual assistance and uplifting one another, while "Namunyak" represents blessing and fortune. Together, our name embodies the spirit of self-determination, community solidarity, and generational stewardship.

Founded by dedicated community leaders in Kajiado county, Ereto Namunyak CBO works hand-in-hand with traditional elders, schools, women groups, and youth to address rural challenges through homegrown, dignity-centered solutions.
